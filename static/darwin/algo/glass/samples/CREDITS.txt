Piano: Salamander Grand Piano V3 (Yamaha C5) by Alexander Holm, licensed CC BY 3.0
       (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). The 30-note MP3 set as prepared for Tone.js (github.com/Tonejs/audio).
Violin: VS Chamber Orchestra: Community Edition (VSCO 2 CE), Solo Violin, Arco Vibrato, p and f layers,
        recorded by Sam Gossner & Simon Dalzell, Versilian Studios; CC0 1.0. Trimmed to 6 s with a fade and encoded as MP3.
Pipe organ: VSCO 2 CE Keys/Organ (quiet third manual, loud pedal), sampled by Simon Dalzell of Ivy Audio; CC0.
Double bass: VSCO 2 CE Solo Contrabass, sustain without vibrato, two dynamics; CC0.
Percussion: VSCO 2 CE snare (snares on) hits and rolls, bass drum, five timpani hits and rolls (pitches measured: 41.5, 46.8, 49.5, 52.4, 54.6),
            and VSCO 1 tam-tam and suspended/clash cymbals; CC0.
Choir: epic-choir by thepok (github.com/thepok/epic-choir), derived from VocalSet (Wilkins, Seetharaman, Wahl, Pardo 2018),
       CC BY 4.0, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.1442513. Used: S/A/T/B sections, vowels ah/oh/oo, mf layer, every whole tone;
       trimmed (0.08 s onset offset, 6 s, fade) and encoded mono 80 kbps MP3. Further modified here: re-pitched by playback rate.
Violin and cello (violin2/, cello2/): University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios, Musical Instrument Samples (theremin.music.uiowa.edu),
       "may be downloaded and used for any projects, without restrictions". Arco pp/mf/ff, cut from the chromatic runs, one note
       per whole tone, root pitches measured (autocorrelation) and stored in manifest.json so playback is tuned exactly.
Glass Ensemble (ens/): Renaissance organ (Full and 8') and Saxello, VCSL by Versilian Studios, CC0; Flute sustain vibrato,
       VSCO 2 CE, CC0; Finger Bass YR (Yamaha RBX) by Andrea Biasior via FreePats, CC0.
