Discovery Memory · Failure Memory

What Darwin has learned, and what didn't work

A genome enters Discovery Memory only after its claim passes held-out validation. New experiments seed a quarter of their starting population from the most similar memories. Whether that helps is a hypothesis, tested every day on the Intelligence page. Failure Memory keeps what did not work. With failure weighting on, memories that keep failing to transfer are tried less often, but never ruled out.
