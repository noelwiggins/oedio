Integrity rules in force

  • World simulation is genuine: no scripted behaviour, no fabricated generations. Re-simulations and replays are labelled as such.
  • Training and held-out seeds are disjoint by construction; claims move to replicated only on held-out evidence (≥ 7/8 wins, one-sided sign test p ≤ 0.05).
  • Stopping is never treated as success; every stop reason is recorded.
  • The Ledger and claim-status history are append-only (database triggers) and hash-chained (SHA-256).
  • Visitor goal text and imported text are untrusted. The LLM assessor has no tools, gets the goal as quoted data, and only validated JSON is accepted. Proposed configs are re-validated against limits.
  • Exports redact anything that looks like a credential; visitors are identified only by a salted hash.
